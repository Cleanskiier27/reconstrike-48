# Judge Rubric

## Weighted Criteria
- **Accuracy:** 40%
- **Depth/Completeness:** 25%
- **Detection Quality:** 20%
- **Reproducibility/Documentation:** 10%
- **Innovation:** 5%

## Penalties
- Unsafe/non-isolated execution
- Unverifiable findings
- Missing evidence chain
- Plagiarized or fabricated content

## Tie-Breakers
1. Detection quality
2. Reproducibility
3. Clarity of evidence
